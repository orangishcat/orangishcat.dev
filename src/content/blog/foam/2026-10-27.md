Today I created a custom SQLite collation.

Collations are comparison functions, and I specifically used them for using natural sort on the UI screens.

```rust
connection.create_collation("NATURAL_ORDER", |a, b| natord::compare_ignore_case(a, b))
```

The `natord` crate already handles comparison cleanly, and `rusqlite` made it easy to create a collation.

Then I just had to replace my `ORDER BY collection COLLATE NOCASE` statements with `COLLATE NATURAL_ORDER`.
